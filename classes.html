Class Topics

Nursery – Year II

  • 🌈 **Colors & Shapes** – Basic colors (red, blue, yellow...) and simple shapes (circle, square, triangle)
  • 🔤 **Alphabets Recognition** – A to Z identification with sounds and flashcards
  • 🔢 **Counting 1-20** – Number recognition and counting with objects
  • 🎵 **Rhymes & Songs** – English and Urdu rhymes for listening and speaking
  • 📘 **Story Time** – Simple moral stories with visuals and actions
  • ✋ **Fine Motor Skills** – Activities like coloring, clay molding, tracing patterns
  • 🧍‍♂️ **Personal Hygiene & Manners** – Brushing teeth, washing hands, saying thank you/sorry
  • 🌞 **Seasons & Weather** – Introduction to sun, rain, winter, summer through fun charts
  • 🐶 **Animals & Sounds** – Domestic and wild animals with sounds and movement
  • 🏠 **Myself & My Home** – Learning about family members, rooms, and personal info

Year III – Year VI

  • 🔡 Phonics & Word Building – CVC words (cat, bat), blending & segmenting sounds
  • 📖 Reading & Comprehension – Simple short stories with picture-based questions
  • ✍️ Creative Writing – My School, My Family, My Pet (5-line writing)
  • 🔢 Numbers 1–100 – Place value, comparison (>, <), even/odd numbers
  • ➕ Basic Operations – Addition and subtraction with pictures and number lines
  • ⏰ Time & Calendar – Days of the week, months, reading clock (o’clock)
  • 🧼 Personal Safety & Hygiene – Stranger danger, healthy habits, safety signs
  • 🌿 Basic Science – Living vs non-living things, parts of plant, body parts
  • 🌍 My Country & World – National flag, famous places, continents/oceans intro
  • 🎨 Art & Craft – Paper folding, coloring within borders, cutting and pasting

Year VII – Year IX

  • 📘 English Language – Grammar (tenses, active/passive), creative essays, letter writing
  • 📚 English Literature – Short stories, poetry analysis, character discussions
  • 🧮 Mathematics – Fractions, decimals, algebra basics, geometry (angles, shapes)
  • 🔬 General Science – Human body systems, matter & states, ecosystems, simple machines
  • 🌎 Geography – Maps & globes, climate zones, natural resources, landforms
  • 📜 History – Early civilizations, Islamic history, major world events
  • 🖥️ Computer Studies – MS Office basics, Internet safety, Introduction to coding (Scratch or Python)
  • 📖 Islamiat – Seerat-un-Nabi ﷺ, Hadith, Quranic surahs & tafseer (basic), Islamic values
  • 💬 Urdu – Mazmoon, Inshaiya, Muhawray, Nazm aur Ghazal ka mutala
  • 🎨 Art & Expression – Poster making, slogan writing, sketching, calligraphy

O Level & A Level

  • 📘 English Language & Literature – Essay writing, comprehension, Shakespeare/modern plays, unseen poetry analysis
  • 🧮 Mathematics – Algebra, Trigonometry, Probability & Statistics, Calculus (A Level), Coordinate Geometry
  • 🧪 Physics – Forces & motion, Energy, Electricity & Magnetism, Modern Physics, Practical-based problem solving
  • 🧫 Chemistry – Atomic structure, Chemical bonding, Moles & Equations, Organic chemistry, Titrations
  • 🧬 Biology – Cell biology, Genetics, Human physiology, Ecology, Biotechnology (A Level)
  • 🌍 Geography – Natural hazards, Population studies, Economic development, GIS & mapping techniques
  • 📜 History – World Wars, Cold War, Pakistan Studies, Source-based questions, Historical interpretations
  • 📊 Business Studies / Economics – Business structure, Marketing, Demand & supply, Inflation, Economic systems
  • 💻 Computer Science – Programming concepts (Python/Java), Databases, Computer architecture, Boolean logic
  • 🕌 Islamiyat & Pakistan Studies – Quranic passages, Life of Prophet ﷺ, Development of Pakistan, Constitutional history
