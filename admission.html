Rules & Regulations

  • Students must attend at least 90% of all classes each term.
  • Uniform is compulsory for all students on all working days.
  • Late arrival or absenteeism must be informed to the administration.
  • Mobile phones and electronic devices are not allowed during class hours.
  • Respecting teachers, staff, and fellow students is mandatory.
  • All school property must be used with care and respect.
  • Disciplinary action may be taken for breaking school rules.
